# 4. Observe, measure, diagnost, explain
- The raw propagation reproduced the previous result pretty much exactly the raw final error was -0.011309 rad exactly as expected.
- The corrected estimate saw a reduction in the final error relative to the raw final error (-0.011309 rad vs -0.000370 rad)
- Raw final error: -0.011309 rad (-0.647973 deg), Corrected final error: -0.000370 rad (-0.021196 deg)
- The innovation represents the difference between the predicted result and the observed measurement. In this case it is the difference between the predicted yaw value using just the gyro and the measured odometry from the sim.
- It implies we trust the odometry measurement more than our gyro sensor readings, the larger the correction gain the more we move the prediction towards the odometry measurement and away from the pure gyro sensor estimate.
- The system isn't static and is in motion so comparing updated sensor readings against old observations will introduce error into the estimator.
- Odometry can have its own sources of error, this can introduce biases against a genuine truth value.
